Illuminating Mind & Soul

Indoor Cultural & Rainy-Day Activities

Bangladesh is known for a lot of things. There are plenty of enriching indoor activities for a rainy day in Bangladesh that are illuminating for both the mind and soul.

1971 Independence

Liberation War Museum

A monument to Bangladeshi independence and the 1971 war fought for freedom. Detailed English explanatory boards, archival photographs, rare documents, and documentary videos provide deep insight into the nation's struggle for nationhood.

Agargaon, Dhaka Inquire Visit →
Lost-Wax Heritage

Sukanta's Dhamrai Metal Crafts

Extraordinary traditional brass and bronze handicrafts! A must-visit heritage home and workshop in Dhamrai where a welcoming artisan family demonstrates centuries-old metal casting craftsmanship firsthand.

Dhamrai, Dhaka Book Workshop →
Underground Museum

Independence Museum

The first and only underground museum in Bangladesh, tracing the nation's history from the Mughal era to independence in 1971. Located inside the 67-acre Suhrawardy Udyan complex where the historic March 7 speech and 1971 surrender took place.

Suhrawardy Udyan, Dhaka Plan Tour →
Photography & Art

Drik Gallery, Dhaka

Premier visual arts and documentary photography exhibition venue in Dhaka. Features upscale, centrally air-conditioned exhibition halls, well-marked gallery stalls, a curated library, and a cozy artist coffee shop.

Panthapath, Dhaka Check Exhibitions →
Space & Planetarium

Novo Theatre

Opened on 25 September 2004, this iconic space center features sharp angular architecture contrasting with a central futuristic planetarium dome. Houses astronomy exhibits, virtual simulator rides, 5D shows, and a ground-floor cafe.

Bijoy Sarani, Dhaka Show Timings →
