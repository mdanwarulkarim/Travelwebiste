Local Mobility

Getting Around Bangladesh Like a Local

Navigate bustling city streets, tranquil river deltas, and steep mountain tracks using these signature modes of transport.

Urban & Heritage

Cycle Rickshaw & Metro

Colorful hand-painted cycle rickshaws are the heart of neighborhood travel in Dhaka, while the modern Dhaka Metro Rail (MRT Line-6) beats city traffic in minutes.

Hill Tracts Exclusive

Chander Gari (Moon Car)

Open-top four-wheel-drive utility jeeps specially modified to climb the steep, winding mountain roads of Sajek Valley, Bandarban, and Khagrachhari.

Riverine Delta

Sadarghat Launches & Sampans

Triple-deck passenger river launches connect Dhaka to Barishal and Bagerhat overnight, while traditional wooden Sampan boats glide across Kaptai Lake and Sangu River.

City & Highway

CNG Auto-Rickshaw

Green compressed-natural-gas three-wheelers are ubiquitous across Dhaka, Chittagong, and Sylhet for quick point-to-point hops through busy streets.
